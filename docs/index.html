Questions

Is Textablo free?

Yes. Notes, the board and everything on this page are free. An optional Plus may come later for people who want more.

Where are my notes stored?

On your PC, in Textablo’s own folder. Sync through OneDrive or Google Drive only happens if you switch it on.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Textablo is for Windows 10 and 11.

How do I move from Notion or Evernote?

In Textablo, open Settings, then Data, then Import notes. Your existing notes stay as they are.

What if something doesn’t work?

Use Help, then Report a problem, inside the app. Reports reach a person, privately.
