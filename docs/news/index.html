What’s new

Every change in every version of Textablo.

1.2.122026-10-10

  • NewA new button at the bottom right of a board: the whole board as a picture (PNG), as a vector picture (SVG, where text stays text), or a snapshot of just what you see right now.
  • ImprovedSelected things on a board, and pictures in a document, get one thin line and four identical corner handles, sharp at any zoom.
  • ImprovedText on a board is exactly as big as what you wrote, while you type and after.
  • ImprovedA document is as wide as a sheet of paper, and sits in the middle when there is room.
  • ImprovedClipboard history is now off until you turn it on, and before it starts it tells you exactly what it keeps and where.
  • ImprovedThe frames panel says what frames are for, and a frame drawn too small becomes a normal size.
  • popravkaWhen a document with several headings becomes a board for the first time, its cards no longer overlap.
  • popravkaThe document card in split view is no longer faded, the count on the trash sits in the middle, “Export” no longer appears twice in the menu, plus more small touches.

1.2.112026-10-09

  • NewNew in the sidebar: “All boards” shows all your boards as pictures, favorites first. In the notes list, a board shows a little picture of itself too.
  • NewA button at the top left of a note hides the notes list, so a board gets the whole window. It’s remembered separately for boards and documents.
  • ImprovedWhen saving fails (a full disk, a locked folder), a bar stays until it’s fixed, and closing asks before losing changes. A sync folder that isn’t available, a file held by OneDrive, and conflict copies from Dropbox or Google Drive are now handled safely.
  • NewHelp → Achievements: 33 achievements for writing, notes, boards, tasks and habits, and Textabby’s journey through 10 levels. It’s all counted on your PC. A few of them are secret…
  • ImprovedA big security pass: secrets are always encrypted, the error log never holds keys or personal details, updates and going back a version check the signature, and in a sync folder Textablo touches only its own files.
  • ImprovedA picture pasted from a website is downloaded once and kept locally; Textablo never loads pictures straight from the web, so no site learns when you open a note.
  • NewSettings → General → Links: titles and pictures for pasted links can be turned off, so no site is contacted.
  • NewWhen the terms of use change, Textablo tells you once and asks you to accept them.
  • ImprovedToday’s note is kept only once you write in it; open it and leave, and it doesn’t show up in your list.

1.2.102026-10-04

  • NewBefore anything is deleted, Textabby asks yes or no, with “No” selected, so a stray click or Enter never deletes anything. This covers notes, templates, reminders, calendars and the clipboard history.
  • NewSettings → Data: one button (with two confirmations) removes everything Textablo keeps on this computer, and the app starts fresh.
  • NewThe terms of use are in the app (Settings → About → Terms) and on textablo.com. The privacy policy is updated too.
  • ImprovedThe arrow at the bottom of the sidebar folds it to icons and back.
  • ImprovedTextabby the turtle greets you in the tour and in empty lists; the welcome note and board are in English.
  • ImprovedShared notes now open at textablo.com/n/, and the page has “Report this note”. Older links keep working.
  • ImprovedIn Windows contrast themes, focus, window edges and switched-on buttons stay visible.
  • FixedA note pinned to the desktop no longer loses “always on top” when it opens while another window has focus.

  • FixedWhile you pick part of the screen, Textablo stays on the taskbar instead of vanishing.

1.2.22026-09-30

  • NewFocus (in the left menu): say how long you want to work and Textablo plans the work and the breaks (Pomodoro 25/5, 52/17, 90/20 or your own rhythm) and tells you when it's time to rest. A ticking mechanical clock or a digital one, tick-tock if you like, plus a widget: a small clock above all windows that stays when you minimize Textablo. Drag it anywhere (it snaps to edges and corners), switch it to digital or to a small bar with the time. On a break it guides your breathing, and it counts your days in a row.
  • ImprovedFaster with lots of notes: with 5,000 notes Textablo opens twice as fast, search is about 20 times faster, and it uses less memory.
  • ImprovedA reminder in however long you want: type “45 s”, “7 min”, “1h 30m” or “2:30” and Textablo shows the exact time right away. It rings on the second.
  • ImprovedA new, refined logo: clearly visible on both light and dark taskbars, and sharp beside the clock.
  • ImprovedSimpler notifications: a reminder shows only what, in which note and when, and the “Done” and “Snooze 10 min” buttons work right from the notification.
  • NewCalendar (in the left menu): the whole month with your reminders, repeats included ahead. Click a day to see what's on it and add something; drag a reminder onto another day to move it.
  • NewGoogle Calendar in Textablo: paste your calendar's secret iCal address and your Google events show next to your reminders. No sign-in, read-only, and the address is stored encrypted. Every reminder also has “Add to Google Calendar”.
  • NewPin a note to the desktop (··· menu or right-click): a small window with the note that you can write in, can stay in front of all windows, and stays pinned after a restart.
  • NewType / in a note and pick a block: heading, list, to-do, table, image, reminder, today's date… Typing after / filters (e.g. /tab), Esc leaves the / as text.
  • NewA ⋮⋮ handle beside every paragraph: drag it to move the paragraph, click it to turn it into something else, duplicate or delete it. + adds a new line below. From the keyboard: Ctrl + Shift + ↑/↓.
  • NewToggle sections (click the arrow to hide or show what's inside) and callouts with an icon, for tips, warnings and things that are done.
  • NewLong notes with 3 or more headings get an outline on the right: you see where you are, and a click takes you to a heading.
  • NewA note can have its own icon (an emoji) beside the title: it also shows in the list, on Home and in Quick open, so notes are easier to tell apart. Hover above the title → “Add icon”.
  • popravkaThe reminder count on the bell is no longer cut off at the top.
  • popravkaWhat's new, reports, About and import opened from Settings: X takes you back to Settings, on the same tab, instead of closing everything.
  • ImprovedEverything Textablo makes by itself is in its own folder: the daily backup is no longer in Documents (old backups move over by themselves).
  • ImprovedA smaller, cleaner install: updates are about 5 times smaller, and the app folder has 19 files instead of 73.

  • NewReminders from text: write “call Mark tomorrow at 10” and Textablo offers a reminder in one click. Works in the quick note too. Turn it off in Settings → Reminders.
  • NewShare a note by link: the note is inside the link itself and isn’t stored on any server.
  • NewExport a note to PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML or text; a board as a picture or PDF; a whole folder as a zip.
  • NewSearch with filters (type, tag, folder, date, images, tasks, reminder) and operators like #work or has:tasks; matching words are highlighted.
  • ImprovedA new look for Shortcuts (each one explained, with search), What's new, Reminders and empty lists (such as an empty Trash).
  • ImprovedBoard: the linked card (text from the Document) is edited right on the board with a double-click; every change reaches the document at once, and the pictures stay where they were.
  • ImprovedBoard: “New card”, “New to-do list” and the rest from the right-click menu are just added and can be moved right away; double-click to start writing.
  • popravkaThe frame around a selected card or picture fits it exactly and follows its rounded corners; no second frame over the text while you write.

  • ImprovedBoard: a double-click always starts writing in a card. Double-click a picture to draw on it, a shape to add text, and a curved arrow to straighten it.
  • ImprovedSettings are wider and split into categories. Scrollbars are easier to see and no longer stick out over rounded corners.
  • NewLight and dark theme in one click at the bottom left, or Ctrl + Shift + L. Next to it is the Help menu (tour, what's new, report a bug, shortcuts).
  • NewA new tour on first launch: what Textablo can do, and a choice of light or dark theme.

  • NewTextablo in English: the whole app. Pick the language in Settings (by default it follows Windows).
  • NewSettings → About: privacy policy and open-source licenses.
  • NewReport a bug / idea (Help menu): attach pictures (paste with Ctrl + V, drag them in, or capture the window in one click). The report goes privately to the developer, and “My reports” shows what you sent.
  • NewRight-click selected text → "Send to…": the text stays both here and there, linked live. Check off a task in one place and it's checked everywhere.
  • NewTo-do lists show a progress bar with the percentage done: in the note list, in the note and on board cards.
