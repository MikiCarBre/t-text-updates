What’s new

Every change in every version of Textablo.

1.2.42026-10-02

  • NewNew name and logo. Your notes and settings stay as they are.
  • NewGroup part of a board, give it a title, move it from anywhere inside.
  • NewFrames become full-screen slides. Arrow keys move between them.
  • NewMark a sentence in a PDF and it becomes a card, with the page.
  • NewDrop in another note, or a link with a page preview.
  • NewCtrl+L keeps an item from moving by accident.
  • NewDouble-click a shape to write in it. Cards get text sizes too.
  • NewSee where you are on a big board.
  • NewCtrl+Shift+D opens the note for today.
  • NewMermaid diagrams and KaTeX formulas in notes.
  • NewScan a QR code to see your notes on your phone, on the same Wi-Fi.
  • ImprovedThe intro shows the main idea: one note, two views.
  • popravkaClicks no longer go through, and “To the board” works.
  • popravkaThe link now sits under the PIN field.
  • ImprovedDiagrams can’t load anything from the web; the phone link has limits.

1.2.32026-10-01

  • NewAES-256 on disk, in history, snapshots and backups.
  • NewSets a new PIN if you forget yours, and opens notes on another PC.
  • NewMake a password-protected backup in Settings → Backup.
  • NewCtrl+Shift+K locks every open locked note again.
  • ImprovedLocked notes lock again when the PC sleeps or Windows locks.
  • ImprovedA new PIN needs at least 6 characters; old PINs keep working.

1.2.22026-09-30

  • NewFocus (in the left menu): say how long you want to work and T-Text plans the work and the breaks (Pomodoro 25/5, 52/17, 90/20 or your own rhythm) and tells you when it's time to rest. A ticking mechanical clock or a digital one, tick-tock if you like, plus a widget: a small clock above all windows that stays when you minimize T-Text. Drag it anywhere (it snaps to edges and corners), switch it to digital or to a small bar with the time. On a break it guides your breathing, and it counts your days in a row.
  • ImprovedSecurity: a locked note opens only with the PIN (and can't be copied or exported while locked), guessing the PIN is slowed down, T-Text never turns on the microphone or camera, never opens anything on your home network, and the link with Claude is protected from impostor programs. Damaged or malicious files (calendars, imports) can no longer freeze or crash the app, and "Delete forever" also deletes the note's history.
  • ImprovedFaster with lots of notes: with 5,000 notes T-Text opens twice as fast, search is about 20 times faster, and it uses less memory.
  • ImprovedA reminder in however long you want: type “45 s”, “7 min”, “1h 30m” or “2:30” and T-Text shows the exact time right away. It rings on the second.
  • ImprovedA new, refined logo: clearly visible on both light and dark taskbars, and sharp beside the clock.
  • ImprovedSimpler notifications: a reminder shows only what, in which note and when, and the “Done” and “Snooze 10 min” buttons work right from the notification.
  • NewCalendar (in the left menu): the whole month with your reminders, repeats included ahead. Click a day to see what's on it and add something; drag a reminder onto another day to move it.
  • NewGoogle Calendar in T-Text: paste your calendar's secret iCal address and your Google events show next to your reminders. No sign-in, read-only, and the address is stored encrypted. Every reminder also has “Add to Google Calendar”.
  • NewPin a note to the desktop (··· menu or right-click): a small window with the note that you can write in, can stay in front of all windows, and stays pinned after a restart.
  • NewType / in a note and pick a block: heading, list, to-do, table, image, reminder, today's date… Typing after / filters (e.g. /tab), Esc leaves the / as text.
  • NewA ⋮⋮ handle beside every paragraph: drag it to move the paragraph, click it to turn it into something else, duplicate or delete it. + adds a new line below. From the keyboard: Ctrl + Shift + ↑/↓.
  • NewToggle sections (click the arrow to hide or show what's inside) and callouts with an icon, for tips, warnings and things that are done.
  • NewLong notes with 3 or more headings get an outline on the right: you see where you are, and a click takes you to a heading.
  • NewA note can have its own icon (an emoji) beside the title: it also shows in the list, on Home and in Quick open, so notes are easier to tell apart. Hover above the title → “Add icon”.
  • popravkaThe reminder count on the bell is no longer cut off at the top.
  • popravkaWhat's new, reports, About and import opened from Settings: X takes you back to Settings, on the same tab, instead of closing everything.
  • ImprovedEverything T-Text makes by itself is in its own folder: the daily backup is no longer in Documents (old backups move over by themselves).
  • ImprovedA smaller, cleaner install: updates are about 5 times smaller, and the app folder has 19 files instead of 73.

1.2.12026-09-29

  • ImprovedSecurity: stricter cleaning of content from other people's files, an exported web page can't run scripts, importing can't freeze the app, web images in Claude's replies don't load by themselves, and a bug report doesn't send your Windows user name or file names.
  • popravkaBoard: the eraser removes what it touches, including a frame with a card on top of it and everything along a fast swipe. It leaves cards, pictures and text alone, and Ctrl + Z brings back what was erased. Picking another tool clears the selection.
  • ImprovedBoard: everything is easier to grab. Drag a selected item from anywhere inside it; grab an empty frame or ellipse by its middle; arrows and pen strokes have a wide grip at any zoom. A long word without spaces no longer runs out of its card.
  • NewBoard: every tool has its own color. Pick a tool and choose its color right next to it; it stays for that tool until you pick another, even after a restart.
  • ImprovedBoard: an arrow on a picture points exactly at the detail where you let go. Dragging the arrow itself moves it as its own element.
  • popravkaHighlighted text is readable in the dark theme too. Tidier spacing in Settings and on Home.
  • NewImport notes from Google Keep, Notion, Evernote, Obsidian (Markdown) and Word; OneNote via Word. You see what was found before importing, and importing again makes no duplicates. Settings → Data.
  • NewReminders from text: write “call Mark tomorrow at 10” and T-Text offers a reminder in one click. Works in the quick note too. Turn it off in Settings → Reminders.
  • NewShare a note by link: the note is inside the link itself and isn’t stored on any server.
  • NewExport a note to PDF, Word, Markdown, HTML or text; a board as a picture or PDF; a whole folder as a zip.
  • NewSearch with filters (type, tag, folder, date, images, tasks, reminder) and operators like #work or has:tasks; matching words are highlighted.
  • ImprovedA new look for Shortcuts (each one explained, with search), What's new, Reminders and empty lists (such as an empty Trash).
  • ImprovedBoard: the linked card (text from the Document) is edited right on the board with a double-click; every change reaches the document at once, and the pictures stay where they were.
  • ImprovedBoard: “New card”, “New to-do list” and the rest from the right-click menu are just added and can be moved right away; double-click to start writing.
  • NewBoard: right-clicking text while writing in a card offers bold, italic, strikethrough, highlight, link, heading, lists, checking off a task and Claude.
  • popravkaThe frame around a selected card or picture fits it exactly and follows its rounded corners; no second frame over the text while you write.
  • popravkaHighlighted text across a bold part is one piece, with no gap in the middle.
  • popravkaThe “linked” badge is in English when the app is in English.
  • popravka“Start with Windows” now really turns on.
  • popravkaCards with tasks or linked text no longer have extra space at the bottom; the text sits in the middle of the card.
  • ImprovedBoard: a double-click always starts writing in a card. Double-click a picture to draw on it, a shape to add text, and a curved arrow to straighten it.
  • ImprovedSettings are wider and split into categories. Scrollbars are easier to see and no longer stick out over rounded corners.
  • NewLight and dark theme in one click at the bottom left, or Ctrl + Shift + L. Next to it is the Help menu (tour, what's new, report a bug, shortcuts).
  • NewA new tour on first launch: what T-Text can do, and a choice of light or dark theme.
  • NewHome: a greeting, reminders, tasks from all your notes (check them off right there), a quick note, recent and favorite notes, templates and “Getting started”. Pick what shows under “Customize”.
  • NewT-Text in English: the whole app. Pick the language in Settings (by default it follows Windows).
  • NewSettings → About: privacy policy and open-source licenses.
  • NewReport a bug / idea (Help menu): attach pictures (paste with Ctrl + V, drag them in, or capture the window in one click). The report goes privately to the developer, and “My reports” shows what you sent.
  • NewRight-click selected text → "Send to…": the text stays both here and there, linked live. Check off a task in one place and it's checked everywhere.
  • NewTo-do lists show a progress bar with the percentage done: in the note list, in the note and on board cards.
  • New"What's new": after every update you see what changed, by date.
  • ImprovedNew versions are checked for every 30 minutes (you can change this in Settings).
  • popravkaX buttons and the buttons at the top of the window (Settings, Shortcuts, menus) now always respond to clicks; no more need for Escape.
  • popravkaIn a narrow window, toolbar buttons that don't fit move into the "More" menu instead of disappearing.
  • popravkaJust opening a note or clicking "Edit" no longer changes its modified time or moves it to the top of the list.
  • ImprovedQuick note (Ctrl + Alt + N) has an X to close it.
  • ImprovedBoard: double-clicking an empty spot no longer creates a new card. Double-clicking a card still opens it for writing; new cards are made with the button, the N key or a right-click.
  • popravkaIn a narrow window, dialogs no longer run off the screen, and board tools scroll when there is no room.
  • ImprovedSearch stays fast even with thousands of notes.
  • NewDark theme: Settings → Appearance (Light, Dark or follow Windows). The board, cards and pen follow the theme.
  • ImprovedUpdates are digitally signed: T-Text only installs a release we really published.
  • ImprovedSecurity: notes from someone else's backup or a shared folder are cleaned of anything that could run code, and importing an opened backup asks first.
  • ImprovedKeyboard: Tab stays inside an open window, focus returns where it was, arrow keys work in menus. Better screen reader support.
  • ImprovedFaster start: the board and windows load only when needed. Pictures in long notes load as you scroll to them.
  • ImprovedSaving survives power cuts better, and an antivirus or OneDrive briefly locking the file no longer fails a save.
  • popravkaFolder sync: when two computers change the same note at the same moment, both now keep the same version.
